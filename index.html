Our Voice identity guide

Tone, word rules, and the do/don't guide for writing as @sat_pay
▼

Tone Split

80% Composed Confidence
20% Warmth

We sound like...

Someone who already lives on a Bitcoin Standard and is genuinely surprised more people don't. Not angry about it. Just clear-eyed. Confident. Composed. Bitcoin-native. Premium but never pretentious.

We don't sound like...

StrikeIdeological maximalism. Yelling about fiat. "BTFD" energy.
FoldGamified energy. Spinning wheels. Carnival vibes.
ether.fiMeme-bro tone. Funny as a default, not when it counts.
PlasmaInstitutional boardroom speak. Talking to investors, not people.

Example tweets showing the voice

Your Bitcoin should be doing more than sitting in a wallet.
The whole point of Bitcoin is financial freedom. Hard to feel free when you have to sell it every time you need cash.
Never selling. Always earning. Living on a Bitcoin Standard.

Things we never do

No em dashes. They're an obvious AI tell. Use periods, commas, or restructure.
No self-custody claims. SatPay is not self-custodial. Never imply users hold their own keys.
No DeFi jargon. No "TVL," no "restaking," no "liquidity pools." Talk like a bank, not a protocol.
No desperate engagement farming. No "like if you agree" or "RT for good luck."
No fake scenarios. Don't write "Imagine you need $2,000 for rent..." stories.
No CORE price mentions. Don't make price the focus.
No old-style waitlist CTAs. It's "get early access" or "apply for beta" now.
No philosophy disconnected from product. Ours always connects back to what SatPay does.
